Your image is sent to our server for the time it takes to process, in memory only, and the result is sent back to you. Nothing is written to disk or kept.

🎬 Videos: processed locally

Spectra Video runs entirely in your browser. Your video never leaves your device.

3. Image processing

  • Upload: encrypted in transit (HTTPS).
  • Processing: on our server, in memory, with no copy or backup. Background removal uses an open model running on that server: no third-party AI sees your images.
  • Result: sent straight back to your browser; nothing is kept afterwards.
  • EXIF metadata (camera, GPS location…): removed during conversion. The Rights tool only adds the information you enter to the file.
  • No AI training on your images.

4. What stays on your device

  • Spectra Video: everything happens in your browser, nothing is sent to our servers.
  • Image app: your projects (including the original image), recipes and gradients are saved in your browser so you can pick up your work. We have no access to them; you can erase them by clearing the site's data.
  • Rights tool: your name and contact details are only remembered in your browser if you tick “Remember my identity”.

5. Hosting and service providers

  • Railway: hosts the server that processes images.
  • Vercel: hosts the pages and cookieless audience measurement (if accepted).
  • Sentry: technical error reports, with no personal data.
  • Supabase: database for NOXEL accounts and plans (only if you create an account).
  • Stripe: payment for plans and credits; your card details never reach us.
  • Resend: sends sign-in and confirmation emails.
  • Google: sign-in with a Google account, only if you choose it.
  • Black Forest Labs (FLUX.2): receives the text of your AI drawing requests, never your images.

These services may process data in the United States or Canada. Your data is never sold, rented or shared for advertising.

Frequently asked questions

Are my images kept after processing?

No. An image is sent to our server only for the time it takes to process, in memory only: it is never written to disk or saved, and the result is sent straight back to you.

Are my videos uploaded?

No. NOXEL SPECTRA Video processes everything in your browser. Your video never leaves your device.

Are my files used to train AI models?

No. Your files are never used for training. Background removal uses an open model that runs on our own server.

Which image formats can I export to?

JPEG, PNG, WebP, AVIF and GIF. Vectorization produces an SVG. The Favicons tool generates a set of icons for the web and mobile devices.

What is the maximum image size?

25 MB per image. Batch processing accepts up to 20 images and 100 MB in total.

Is Spectra Video finished?

No, it's in beta. The listed tools work and new features are added regularly. A recent browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge) is recommended.

Do you collect tracking data?

Very little. Our servers keep technical logs for security and debugging. In the app, cookieless audience measurement (Vercel Web Analytics) is only active if you accept it. No Google Analytics, no advertising and no social media cookies on Spectra.

How do I exercise my rights?

Write to legal@noxel360.com with the subject “Rights request” and your request (access, correction, deletion, portability). We reply within 30 days.
