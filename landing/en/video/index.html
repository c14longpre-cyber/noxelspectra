NOXEL SPECTRA Video

NOXEL SPECTRA Video prepares your videos right in your browser. The video stays on your device: nothing is uploaded.

Try Spectra Video (beta)

Beta version: the tools below work and more are being added. A recent browser is required.

Shrink and convert

  • Analyze: the video's format, duration, dimensions, bitrate and tracks.
  • Under 25 MB and Compress: aim for a size in MB or choose the quality.
  • Convert: MP4, WebM or MOV.
  • Ready for my site: a light video, its thumbnail and the embed code.

Trim and frame

  • Trim: keep a clip of the video.
  • Crop and resize: 9:16, 1:1, 4:5 or 16:9.
  • Versions for social media: vertical, square and horizontal versions of the same clip.
  • Rotate and flip, Speed.

Sound, text and rights

  • Audio: mute, adjust the volume, add music.
  • Text and logo: titles and a logo placed on the video.
  • Copyright: rights written into the file and a visible watermark.
  • Thumbnail: a cover image taken from the video.
